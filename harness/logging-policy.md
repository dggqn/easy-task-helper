# Logging and Compression Policy

## During Work

Record task goal, scope, acceptance target, inspected areas, assumptions,
commands and results, failures and retries, changed files, behavior impact,
commit and push status, risks, blockers, and next step. Never record secrets or
unrelated private data.

## After a Milestone

Keep the current milestone log detailed until the node is accepted. When a node
or phase is complete, compress older logs into a concise summary preserving
decisions, results, risks, and follow-up. Keep the current log and latest
compressed summary.
